This is okie talkie

Dear talkies, 😊 😊

okie talkie is an English free talk question generator, a tiny tool made for anyone who enjoys having conversations. It’s built to spark connection—through questions, reflection, and a little bit of curiosity. No fancy features here, just lots of random prompts to get people talking.

Whether you're an English tutor, a language learner, two people getting to know each other, someone exploring your thoughts, or just chatting with a friend—this tool is here for you.

If okie talkie has helped you in any way, I’d love to hear about it. Every little story means a lot. 💛
